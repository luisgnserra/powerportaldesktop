# Power Portal - Privacy policy / Politica de privacidade

Effective date / Vigencia: 2026-11-03

## English

This policy describes the Microsoft Store edition of Power Portal. Its Store
publisher is Luis Gustavo Nascimento Serra. The application credits Excelencia
Consultoria e Educacao for ownership/development. Privacy inquiries can be sent
to contato@consultoriaexcelencia.com.br.

### Local Power BI and project data

Power Portal discovers running local Power BI Desktop instances and reads local
connection information and window titles to let you select a model. After you
select/connect to a model or import a local project, it processes the metadata,
expressions and data requested by the selected tool on your computer. Requested
operations may include sample values, row counts, date ranges, relationships,
refresh timing and status. Model-changing operations ask for confirmation.
Native theme/refresh features automate the selected Power BI window; they do not
give Power Portal an independent cloud service for processing your model.

The application does not implement its own analytics/advertising telemetry
service and does not automatically upload your model, expressions, credentials
or query results to a Power Portal server. This does not change the behavior of
Power BI, your data sources, Windows or the Microsoft Store.

### Local storage and your choices

Language preferences, relationship layouts, selected group images, refresh
history, temporary theme files, and backups of supported model changes can be
stored locally. Exports are saved to a location you choose. Diagnostics/errors
may contain local object names and paths. The Store edition uses a separate
application profile from the EXE edition.

You can choose not to connect/import, stop using a tool, delete exported files
and backups you no longer need, or uninstall the Store edition in Windows.
Uninstalling does not necessarily remove exported files, backups or data outside
the application-managed storage. Contact support if you need help locating local
data before deleting it. Keep backups before changing your Power BI model.

### Email, links and TV Tool

The ideas form opens your default email application with the recipient, subject
and message filled in. It does not send the email itself or confirm delivery.
Review the draft and send it in your email application. Do not enter credentials,
confidential information or personal/model data in the ideas form.

External website, support, training and social links open third-party services
when you choose them. TV Tool can load report/web addresses that you enter or
select in its online preview; exported presentations also load those addresses
when opened. Those services receive network requests and are governed by their
own privacy policies. Do not place secrets in URLs. Power Portal does not bypass
their authentication or permissions.

Updates for this edition are managed by Microsoft Store. Selecting the Store
button opens the Store updates page; this edition does not download the EXE
updater from GitHub.

### Requests and policy changes

If you send a support email, its contents are handled by the support recipient
and email providers to respond to your request. Avoid sending model files or
sensitive data. Contact the address above for privacy questions or requests
about information you have voluntarily sent to support. Updates to this policy
will be published at this same policy address with a revised effective date.

### Dados locais e armazenamento

O aplicativo identifica instancias locais do Power BI Desktop e seus titulos
para permitir a escolha do modelo. Ao conectar ou importar um projeto, processa
no computador os metadados, expressoes e dados solicitados pela ferramenta,
incluindo amostras, contagens, periodos, relacionamentos e desempenho de
atualizacao. Alteracoes no modelo exigem confirmacao. Temas e atualizacao podem
automatizar a janela selecionada do Power BI.

O Power Portal nao implementa um servico proprio de telemetria analitica ou
publicitaria e nao envia automaticamente modelos, credenciais, expressoes ou
resultados para um servidor Power Portal. Isso nao modifica o tratamento de
dados pelo Windows, Microsoft Store, Power BI ou pelas fontes consultadas.

Preferencias, diagramas, imagens de grupos, historico de atualizacao, arquivos
temporarios e backups podem ser armazenados localmente. Exportacoes usam o
destino escolhido por voce. Erros podem conter nomes de objetos e caminhos
locais. O perfil desta edicao e separado do perfil da edicao EXE.

Voce pode nao conectar/importar, excluir exportacoes e backups desnecessarios
ou desinstalar pela configuracao do Windows. A desinstalacao pode nao remover
exportacoes, backups e arquivos fora do armazenamento gerenciado pelo aplicativo.
Solicite orientacao ao suporte antes de remover dados locais se tiver duvidas.

### Email e servicos externos

O formulario de ideias apenas solicita a abertura do seu aplicativo de email
com uma mensagem preenchida. Revise e envie por la; nao ha envio automatico
nem confirmacao de entrega pelo Power Portal. Nao inclua dados confidenciais.

Links externos abrem servicos de terceiros por sua escolha. O TV Tool carrega
enderecos de relatorios/sites informados por voce na previa online; apresentacoes
exportadas tambem os carregam quando abertas. Esses servicos recebem requisicoes
de rede e seguem suas proprias politicas. Nao coloque segredos nas URLs.
Autenticacao e permissoes desses servicos continuam necessarias.

As atualizacoes desta edicao sao gerenciadas pela Microsoft Store, sem executar
o atualizador EXE do GitHub.

Se voce enviar um email ao suporte, o destinatario e os provedores de email
tratam o conteudo para responder a solicitacao. Evite enviar modelos e dados
sensiveis. Use o contato acima para questoes de privacidade ou solicitacoes
sobre informacoes enviadas voluntariamente ao suporte. Alteracoes nesta politica
serao publicadas no mesmo endereco com uma nova data de vigencia.
